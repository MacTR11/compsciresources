# Year 12 (L6) — Homework & Consolidation Plan, 2026/27 (v2)

Week-by-week **homework** and **consolidation** tasks aligned to the L6 CMP scheme of work — now mapped to the exact files in `Course/`. Designed for the ~4.5 hrs/week of independent study (IST/VESPA).

## How it works
- **Homework** = the **Homework sheet for that week's subtopic(s)** — every sheet is /20, has a Part B that *re-tests earlier topics* (spaced retrieval built in), and comes with a separate answer sheet. In `Course/Component N/<Topic>/<Subtopic>/Homework.pdf`.
- **Consolidation** = rounds out the week and *builds confidence in prior topics*: the subtopic **Revision Notes**, **Flashcards**, the subtopic **Worksheet** (quiz), and — at the marked points — a **Topic Mini-Paper**, **End-of-Topic Worksheet** or a cumulative **Recap Checkpoint**.
- **Recap Checkpoints** (in `Course/Recap Checkpoints/`): Recap 1 (1.1–1.2) → week 10 · Recap 2 (1.1–1.3) → Christmas/week 15 · Recap 3 (1.1–1.4) → week 24 · Recaps 4–6 include 1.5/2.2–2.3 content, so hold them for **Year 13** (Recap 3 + Mock Paper 1 are the Year-12 pre-mock tools).
